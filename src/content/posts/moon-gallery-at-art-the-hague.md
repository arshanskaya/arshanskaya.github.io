From September 29th till October 3d I will exhibit my work at [Moon Gallery](http://www.moongallery.eu), joining the _Dreamland_ group exhibition **_Microgravity Show_** during Art The Hague at Fokker Terminal (Binckhorstlaan 249, 2516 BB, The Hague).

You can read more about my project [Moon Gallery Vanishing Point](http://www.moongallery.eu/artist/alexandra-arshanskaya/) and find more information about the art fair at [Moon Gallery at Art The Hague](https://www.moongallery.eu/workshop/microgravity-show-art-the-hague/) and [Art The Hague](https://artthehague.nl/portfolio_page/dreamland/)

My new painting “Vanishing Point 1.0” is especially made for Art The Hague. This and other artworks are for sale and will be on display at the fair and in the catalog. By purchasing them, you support Moon Gallery in a fundraising campaign for the ISS Mission.

I will be at Art The Hague on Wednesday September 29th (the opening night), on Thursday September 30th and on Friday October 1st (in afternoon) and by appointment.

You can get your tickets [here](https://www.yourticketprovider.nl/events/46417-art-the-hague/)  
[Art The Hague program and location](https://artthehague.nl/engels/)

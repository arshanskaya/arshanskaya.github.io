The Nightingale and the Rose (after Oscar Wilde)  
Music, Storytelling, Eurhythmic Dance and Visual Art.  
On 24th of November 2018 at 16:00 at The Hague.

In this performance music, visual imagery, storytelling and eurythmic dance are joined together into one single artistic and inspiring impression, as a result of a collaboration of dedicated professionals from different cultural backgrounds. For this event I will be creating live drawings.

Performers:

Het Eickhorst Ensemble:  
Paulus van Kruijssen: viool  
Juliaan van der Linden: viola  
Arthur de Groot: cello

Alexandra Arshanskaya: Visual Arts

Susanna Akhverdova: Storytelling, Eurythmic Dance and Script
